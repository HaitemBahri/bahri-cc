# Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## Who I am

I'm Haitem Bahri, an individual based in the United Kingdom. I'm responsible for your data (the "controller"). Contact me at [me@bahri.cc](mailto:me@bahri.cc).

This notice follows the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR. The same protections apply to every visitor, wherever you are, including Libya.

## The contact form

When you send a message, I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to reply to you. The legal basis is legitimate interest: answering people who contact me.

The website doesn't store messages. They're delivered by email to my inbox and kept for as long as needed for our correspondence and any work that follows, with no fixed deletion date. I'll delete them if you ask.

## Your IP address

Your IP address is used only to limit how often the contact form can be sent. It's held in memory for up to 10 minutes and is never stored or logged. Cloudflare Turnstile also processes it to check for spam.

## Logs

The contact form's server logs only a request ID, the response status and an error code. They never include your IP address, name, email or message. These logs are kept for 14 days. The website keeps no access logs.

## Cookies and local storage

This site uses no cookies and no tracking. If you choose light or dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage and stays on your device.

## Who processes data for me

- Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosts the website and the contact form's server, and sends the contact email.
- Cloudflare Turnstile: checks contact form submissions for spam.

## International transfers

AWS stores data in London (`eu-west-2`). Cloudflare Turnstile may process data outside the UK and EU, under standard contractual safeguards.

## Your rights

Every visitor can ask to see, correct or delete their data, to restrict or object to how it's used, or to get a copy of it. Email [me@bahri.cc](mailto:me@bahri.cc).

## Complaints

Please contact me first. If you're still not satisfied, UK visitors can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). EU visitors can complain to the ICO or to [their local data protection authority](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
